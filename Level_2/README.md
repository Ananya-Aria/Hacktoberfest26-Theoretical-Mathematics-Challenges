# Level 2: Break

Each task here makes a **claim** , a statement. Your job is to find the example that proves the claim wrong, or to explain why no such example exists. One good counterexample beats a thousand examples that agree.

You need your **Enigma number E** (see the [main README](../README.md#your-enigma-number-e)). The examples use E = 4671, from the username `math-cat7`. Task 2B also uses your map from Level 1, task 1A.

## The tasks

Each task has its own folder. Submit each task as **its own pull request**, with your work in `Level_2/<task-folder>/<your-github-username>/`. Any format is fine: a `solution.md`, a scanned PDF of handwritten pages, or photos.
